Zeros are Artifact-Reduction rejections, and they are per-channel

Artifact Reduction writes 0 rather than a blank when it cannot clean a channel for an epoch. Proven by toggling AR on one recording, same panel: with AR off every derivation is zero exactly 0.12% of the time — identical across all 25, because that is one real 37 s recording gap. With AR on, zero rates range from 2.2% (Right Anterior) to 51.9% (T4‑T6). Only 1.7% of AR‑on zeros are also zero without AR, so 98.3% are AR rejections, not gaps.

Because rejection is channel-selective, null per channel, not per row: all 25 derivations are zero together in only 1.6% of rows, while 61% are partial. A uniform all-channel zero indicates a recording gap; a partial pattern indicates AR. Runs are quantised to the 8 s EpochStep. Columns affected are marked 0=NA below.
