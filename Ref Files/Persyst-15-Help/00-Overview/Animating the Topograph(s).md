# Animating the Topograph(s)

# **Animating the Topograph(s)**

With a Topograph displayed, Select **Paging Bar** from the **View** menu. Use the buttons on the Paging Bar to move the plot's **Origin** (vertical gray line) to the beginning of its range, step it backward or forward, animate (play) the plot, or set the **Paging Options**.
