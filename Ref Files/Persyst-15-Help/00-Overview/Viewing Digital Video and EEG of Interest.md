# Viewing Digital Video and EEG of Interest

# **Viewing Digital Video and EEG of Interest**

#### Select the Video Player tab on the upper-right corner of the Persyst program window. Use the Pin button to keep the Video Player window displayed.

Click the **Play** button. This will begin normal speed playback of the video file. The Persyst cursor will track the time of the current video frame being displayed overlaid on the EEG data. Persyst will page forward when the cursor reaches the far right of the screen.

#### **Move to a point of time of interest in the EEG**

If the VideoPlayer is still playing, click the Video Player Pause button. Drag the Persyst thumb bar to a position of interest in the EEG. Note that any EEG movements position the Video file to the same point in time in the video file.
