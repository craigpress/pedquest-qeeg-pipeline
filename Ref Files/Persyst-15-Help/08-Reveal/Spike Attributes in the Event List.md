# Spike Attributes in the Event List

### **Spike Attributes in the Event List**

The Event List includes the following attributes for each detected Spike Event:

- Time - the peak of the Focal Spike.
- Sign - either "-" or "+" indicating that the Focal Spike is scalp negative or scalp positive respectively.
- Perception - the perception value of the Focal Spike, ranging from 0 to 1.
- Focus - the channel of the Focal Spike.
- Duration - the duration (FHW+SHW) of the Focal Spike in milliseconds.
- Height - the absolute uV height of the Focal Spike (max(FHW, SHW)).
- Angle - the absolute angle in degrees of the peak of the Focal Spike.
