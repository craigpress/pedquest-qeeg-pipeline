# Legend Bar

# **Legend Bar**

Similar to its use on the EEGPage, the Legend Bar can be displayed and will either show the y-axis range or the trend value at the Caret Time. Right-click on the Legend Bar to toggle between showing the range or the trend value on the Legend Bar.
