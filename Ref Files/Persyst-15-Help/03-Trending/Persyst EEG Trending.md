# Persyst EEG Trending

# **Persyst EEG Trending**

Persyst 15 trending allows staff to see
the timing of detected seizures in context with other changes in the EEG.
Persyst 15 automatically removes most types of electrode
and physiological
artifacts, making trends easier to review and more representative of cerebral activity.
