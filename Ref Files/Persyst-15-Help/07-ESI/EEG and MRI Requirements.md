# EEG and MRI Requirements

# EEG and MRI Requirements for Persyst ESI

# I. EEG data formats and requirements:

# -There is no limitation on EEG duration, but for best results with 10-20 EEG records, it is recommended to include at least one night in the recording. -EEG recordings of any channel count adhering to the 10-20 or 10-10 system are accepted. -EEG records must have a minimum of 18 channels. -High density EEG from EGI nets are accepted, specifically the 128-channel or 256-channel HCGSN nets. -It is recommended the EEG record be processed by Persyst prior to upload to Persyst ESI powered by Epilog to facilitate the user selection of spikes

**II. MRI data formats and requirements:**  
  
     - Persyst ESI powered by Epilog accepts T1, T2 or FLAIR Anatomical Images in DICOM

       (\*.dcm) or NIfTI (\*.nii) formats.  
     - The whole head of the patient must be fully captured in axial, sagittal and coronal planes. A

       good measure of this is that the imaging includes the foramen magnum.  
     - The resolution of the MRI must be equal to or smaller than 1.5 by 1.5 by 1.5 mm  
     - The MRI must be artifact free (no aliasing or metal artifacts).
