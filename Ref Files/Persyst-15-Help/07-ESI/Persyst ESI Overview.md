# Persyst ESI Powered by Epilog

EEG Electrical Source Imaging is a neuroimaging modality that uses a patient’s EEG data co-registered with their MRI to estimate the cortical sources giving rise to the EEG signals of interest measured at the scalp surface. Refer to

https://www.persyst.com/technology/electrical-source-imaging/

for more details.

Persyst ESI powered by Epilog is an integrated solution within the Persyst software platform providing ESI as a service. Persyst ESI facilitates the deidentification and upload of EEG and MRI data to the secure Persyst ESI powered by Epilog AWS cloud. Data are securely processed and ESI results made available in both volumetric and PDF format.
