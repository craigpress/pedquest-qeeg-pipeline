# Create A New Montage

### **Create a** **New Montage**

From the EEG View, select the Montage button on the
EEG Button Bar and then select **Create
Montage**. Choose whether
you want to create a bipolar or referential montage and then begin selecting
your channels. (You can toggle the
bipolar setting; it just determines
whether one or two grids are required per channel.)

Insert
a
separator between the channels to visually group
subsets.
