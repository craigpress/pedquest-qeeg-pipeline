# Move Around the EEG

### **Move Around the EEG**

There are numerous ways to move around the EEG, and which method you use
will depend on what you know about the event(s) of interest.

Your
current position in the EEG is always displayed by the position of the scroll
bar button (thumb bar) at
the bottom of the EEG waveform window.
Use
Page Up/Page Down on your keyboard to page through the EEG, or click to
the left or right of the EEG scrollbar button.

Clicking
on a comment in the Comment
List will move the EEG page to the location
of the comment in the record.

You
can also use the trend to move to a point of interest in the record. Select
the **Show Trends** or
**Split Screen** button on the System Button
Bar. Click on the trend to move the EEG to that point of interest. Select
the Show EEG to view the waveforms.

**Automatic
EEG paging****:**
Select
**Show EEG** or
**Split Screen** from the System Button Bar
and then click on the EEG Window. Press the Spacebar to start and stop
the automatic paging.
