# Get Patient Information

### **Get Patient Information**

View
the
demographic
information recorded about the patient by clicking on the **Show** **Patient**
button.
Click on the **Show EEG**
button
to redisplay the EEG, **Show Trends**
to redisplay the trend display, or **Split Screen**
to show the trends together with the EEG waveforms.

**Only the information that is entered into the Reader and Memo fields and the Status check boxes for Ready, Reviewed and Archived** **is** **saved.**
These fields are optional and the information that is entered is displayed in the
Persyst 14
database.

Reader: Enter the name of the reader:

Memo: This is a free-text field to enter any memos to be displayed in the
Persyst 15 database.

Ready: Select this box to display a “Ready” status in the
Persyst 15 database.

Reviewed: Select this box to display a “Reviewed” status in the
Persyst 15 database.

Archived: Select this box to display an “Archived” status in the
Persyst 15 database.
