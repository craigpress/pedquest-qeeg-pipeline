# Persyst Imaging Workflow

# **Persyst Imaging Workflow**

Persyst includes the Persyst Imaging Workflow (PIW), a suite of functions for interacting with neuroimaging to aid EEG interpretation by qualified clinicians. For example, PIW can be used to align two different imaging volumes (e.g., an MRI and CT scan), to interactively view those volumes, to tag actual or intended intracranial electrode locations, and to export those electrode locations as a new imaging series in DICOM format. PIW organizes pairs of aligned image volumes into “studies.” Note that PIW is not intended to provide diagnostic information. Review of the source images, EEG, and other patient information is recommended.
